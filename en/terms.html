06How services are provided

Each project is documented in a separate agreement or proposal that sets out the scope of work, stages, timelines, cost and acceptance procedure. The terms of a specific agreement prevail over the general provisions of this section.

The general workflow:

  • Briefing and estimation — clarifying the task, goals and constraints; preparing a proposal.
  • Agreement — fixing the scope, cost and timelines in a contract.
  • Delivery in stages — handing over interim results for review.
  • Revisions — the number of revision rounds at each stage is defined by the contract.
  • Acceptance — handover of final materials and source files.

The Client undertakes to provide the necessary materials, access and feedback in a timely manner. Delays on the Client’s side shift the delivery timelines accordingly.
